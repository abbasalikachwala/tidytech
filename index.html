Clear boundaries

Your privacy matters.

Good technology support should feel safe and transparent. You remain in control throughout the appointment.

  • You enter your own passwords, PINs and security codes
  • I never operate your online banking
  • Backups are checked before resets or reinstalls
  • Approval is required before destructive changes
  • Temporary customer data is kept to a minimum
  • Uncertain outcomes are explained honestly
  • No password or security bypassing
  • No advanced data recovery or hardware repair

Common questions

Before you book.

What should I include in my email?

Your name, Christchurch suburb, device and model if known, what is going wrong, any error message, when it started, whether important files are backed up, and a few preferred appointment times.

Do I need to send you my password?

No. Never email passwords, PINs, MFA codes or recovery codes. You enter sensitive credentials yourself during the session.

When are appointments available?

Tidy Tech operates by prior booking, mainly during evenings and weekends. It is not an emergency or 24/7 support service.

Which Christchurch areas do you visit?

Home visits are available within the normal Christchurch service area. Include your suburb in the enquiry so availability can be confirmed before booking.

What if the problem cannot be fixed?

You are paying for professional diagnosis and troubleshooting time. If a fix is not practical, I’ll explain what was found and recommend the most sensible next step.
