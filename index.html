Common questions

Before you get in touch.

What should I include in my email?

Tell me the device, what is going wrong, what you have already tried and a few times that suit you. If you want an onsite visit, include your Christchurch suburb. Do not send passwords, PINs or security codes.

How much does remote support cost?

Remote IT support is NZ$50 for the first hour, with a one-hour minimum charge. If more time is needed, it is NZ$25 for each additional 30 minutes, and I will ask before continuing.

Will I be made to feel silly for not knowing something?

No. Technology is frustrating when it does not work, and nobody knows everything. You will get patient help and a clear explanation without judgement or jargon.

Do I need to send you my password?

No. Never email passwords, PINs, multi-factor authentication codes or recovery codes. You enter sensitive credentials yourself during the session.

When are appointments available?

Tidy Tech works by prior booking, mainly during evenings and weekends. It is not an emergency or 24/7 support service.

Which Christchurch areas do you visit?

Onsite visits are available within the normal Christchurch service area. Include your suburb in the enquiry and I will confirm availability before booking.

What if the problem cannot be fixed?

You are paying for professional diagnosis and troubleshooting time. If a fix is not practical, I will explain what was found and recommend the most sensible next step.
